3. Theory Chunk

💡

Pointing Nouns

In Arabic, to say "This is a...", you use هَٰذَا (Haatha) for masculine words, and هَٰذِهِ (Haathihi) for feminine words.

Masculine
This is a book.
هَٰذَا كِتَابٌ
Feminine
This is a car.
هَٰذِهِ سَيَّارَةٌ
