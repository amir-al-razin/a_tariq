Pillar 02 • Pedagogical Discovery & Esho Arbi Shikhi Logic

The Harakat Fading Finite State Machine (FSM)

Direct Method Breakthrough

Breaking the "Illusion of Fluency"

Traditional apps leave full vowel marks turned on forever. This traps the student in mechanical phonetic decoders rather than syntactic thinkers.

Maulana Misbah systematically fades diacritics in Esho Arbi Shikhi Vol 1. By pruning predictable grammatical symbols, learners transition from relying on diacritics to recognizing structural morphological patterns (صرف) and grammatical syntax (نحو / تركيب).

"When harakat drop, syntactic intuition awakens."

The 4 Golden Calibration States 100% Harakat Preserved

In precisely these four learning domains, full vowelization is strictly maintained regardless of student level:

1. Vocabulary Acquisition Tables

First-time word introductions require definitive, unambiguous phonetic pronunciation and triliteral root mapping.

2. First Noun-Adjective Pairing

Initial examples of Mawsoof-Sifat to clearly reinforce gender and case ending (إعراب) alignment.

3. Verb Conjugation Paradigms (صرف)

Sarf conjugation matrices where subtle vowel shifts change grammatical active/passive voice and aspect.

4. Phonemic Liaison (إلتقاء الساكنين)

Meeting of two silent phonemes requiring vocalic bridges (e.g., kasrah transitions across words).

Immutable Engine Invariants

Two symbols defy all harakat stripping across all 5 stages:

1. Shaddah Preservation: Essential structural doubling gemination. Never stripped.
2. Homograph Disambiguation: Permanent retention of أَنْتَ vs أَنْتِ (with inviolable Ta-Kasra!).

Pillar 03 • Permanent UI Codification

Design System: "Raw Neutral" Specifications

1. Core Philosophy & Tone DESIGN_SYSTEM_RAW_NEUTRAL.md

Dignified Serenity vs. Gamified Cartoons

We permanently rejected Duolingo-style neon color palettes (bright greens, yellow badges, bouncy shadows, cartoon mascots) in favor of a mature, tranquil grayscale minimalism. Sacred Quranic scripture and classical grammar mechanics demand an atmosphere of intellectual focus, not dopamine-driven distractions.

The Zero Border & Zero Shadow Mandate

Prohibited utilities: border-*, divide-*, shadow-*.

How separation is engineered: 100% via background tone shifts (bg-black canvas $\to$ bg-neutral-900 outer container $\to$ bg-[#141413] nested well) paired with generous structural padding and grid gaps.

Canvas & Headers Pure Black (#000000) slate with 80% opacity backdrop-blur sticky headers.
Functional Accents Color strictly reserved for binary quiz confirmations (emerald correct states).
2. Geometry & Diacritic Cushions

Squircles, Pills & RTL Vertical Rhythm

Our architecture enforces a strict geometric duality that communicates component function without instructions or decorative labels:

rounded-3xl / rounded-2xl Passive Squircles

Used for flashcard wrappers, content modules, grammar rule cards, and nested data rows.

rounded-full (Pill) Interactive Pills

Exclusively reserved for primary user controls, translation toggles, and action targets with inverted contrast.

Typography Tokens & Kasrah Buffers
Arabic Scripture & Vocab Cairo (font-arabic)
UI Controls & Admin Quicksand (font-english)
Diacritic Line-Height Cushion leading-[2.4] + py-2
"Generous line-height cushions ensure ascenders (maddah) and descenders (kasrah) are never severed."
