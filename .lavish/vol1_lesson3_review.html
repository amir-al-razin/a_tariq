Step 05 • GRAMMAR_RULE

Vocative Particle (Ya / يَـا)

Grammar Rule

When addressing someone directly using يَـا (O... / Calling Particle), a proper name loses its Tanween and takes only a single Damma (ُ ).

رَاشِدٌ ← يَـا رَاشِدُ
Rashidun becomes Ya Rashidu
وَلَدٌ ← يَـا وَلَدُ
Waladun becomes Ya Waladu
مُعَلِّمٌ ← يَـا مُعَلِّمُ
Mu'allimun becomes Ya Mu'allimu

Step 10 • GRAMMAR_RULE

Alif in Imra'atun (اِمْرَأَةٌ)

Phonetics & Pronunciation

The starting Alif in اِمْرَأَةٌ (woman) is a **Hamzat ul-Wasl** (silent connecting Alif). When preceded by another word like هِيَ, it merges in speech: هِيَ مْرَأَةٌ (Hiya-mra'atun).

اِمْرَأَةٌ غَنِيَّةٌ
Isolated (Alif Pronounced)

"Imra'atun ghaniyyatun" — A rich woman

هِيَ مْرَأَةٌ فَقِيْرَةٌ
Connected in Speech (Alif Silent)

"Hiya-mra'atun faqiratun" — She is a poor woman
