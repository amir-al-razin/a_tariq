Component 22 • Graphic Rule Digest (LingoDeer Learning Tips)

LingoDeer Learning Tip Card

Book Mapping: Page 27 Definite Article

Book Fit: LingoDeer's most acclaimed feature: clean, structured grammar cards before drills so learners never rely on blind guesswork.

Indefinite (نَكِرَة) vs. Definite (مَعْرِفَة)

In Arabic, an indefinite noun ends with Tanween (double vowel mark: ـٌ), meaning "a" or "an". When the definite article اَلْـ ("the") is attached, the Tanween disappears and becomes a single Damma (ـُ).

Textbook Page 27 Formula:
اَلْـ + كِتَابٌ = اَلْكِتَابُ
"The" + "A book" = "The book"
Golden Grammatical Rule:

The prefix اَلْـ and the ending Tanween (ـٌ) are mutually exclusive. They NEVER appear together on the same word! Saying اَلْكِتَابٌ is strictly forbidden in Arabic grammar.
