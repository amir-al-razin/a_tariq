### **Reading Passages & Phrases** [1, 2]
This section demonstrates how plural non-human objects are treated grammatically as feminine singular.

**Demonstratives with Plural Nouns:** [1]
*   **هٰذَا كِتَابٌ** (This is a book) – **هٰذِهِ كُتُبٌ** (These are books)
*   **ذٰلِكَ قَلَمٌ** (That is a pen) – **تِلْكَ أَقْلَامٌ** (Those are pens)
*   **هٰذِهِ سَاعَاتٌ** (These are watches)
*   **تِلْكَ نَظَّارَاتٌ** (Those are glasses)
*   **هٰذِهِ بُيُوتٌ** (These are houses)
*   **هٰذِهِ أَبْوَابٌ** (These are doors)
*   **تِلْكَ أَسْمَاكٌ** (Those are fishes)
*   **تِلْكَ سُفُنٌ** (Those are ships)
*   **هٰذِهِ أَعْلَامٌ** (These are flags)
*   **هٰذِهِ أَحْذِيَةٌ** (These are shoes)
*   **تِلْكَ أَشْجَارٌ** (Those are trees)
*   **هٰذِهِ أَوْرَاقٌ** (These are leaves)
*   **هٰذِهِ سَيَّارَاتٌ** (These are cars)
*   **تِلْكَ مُدُنٌ** (Those are cities)
*   **هٰذِهِ غُرَفٌ** (These are rooms)

**Adjectives with Plural Nouns:** [2]
*   **كِتَابٌ جَدِيدٌ** (A new book) – **كُتُبٌ جَدِيدَةٌ** (New books)
*   **قَلَمٌ جَيِّدٌ** (A good pen) – **أَقْلَامٌ جَيِّدَةٌ** (Good pens)
*   **سَاعَةٌ ثَمِينَةٌ** (A precious watch) – **سَاعَاتٌ ثَمِينَةٌ** (Precious watches)
*   **بَيْتٌ جَمِيلٌ** (A beautiful house) – **بُيُوتٌ جَمِيلَةٌ** (Beautiful houses)
*   **قِصَّةٌ عَجِيبَةٌ** (A wonderful story) – **قِصَصٌ عَجِيبَةٌ** (Wonderful stories)
*   **بَابٌ وَاسِعٌ** (A wide door) – **أَبْوَابٌ وَاسِعَةٌ** (Wide doors)
