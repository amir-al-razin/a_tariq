### **Verb Conjugation Tables**
This lesson introduces the "Lam of Purpose" (لِـ) attached to the present tense verb, which places the verb in the subjunctive mood (fatha ending). The tables demonstrate its usage across different pronouns [3]:

**Base Verb (Masdar):** القِرَاءَةُ (For reading / to read)
*   **لِيَقْرَأَ** (So he reads / Let him read)
*   **لِتَقْرَأَ** (So she reads / Let her read)
*   **لِتَقْرَأَ** (So you [m.] read)
*   **لِتَقْرَئِي** (So you [f.] read)
*   **لِأَقْرَأَ** (So I read)

**Usage in Past Tense Sentences:**
*   جَلَسَ شَاهِدٌ لِيَقْرَأَ (Shahed sat to read)
*   جَلَسَتْ فَاطِمَةُ لِتَقْرَأَ (Fatima sat to read)
*   أَنْتَ جَلَسْتَ لِتَقْرَأَ (You [m.] sat to read)
*   أَنْتِ جَلَسْتِ لِتَقْرَئِي (You [f.] sat to read)
*   أَنَا جَلَسْتُ لِأَقْرَأَ (I sat to read)

**Usage in Present Tense Sentences:**
*   يَجْلِسُ مَاجِدٌ ...... لِيَقْرَأَ (Majid sits... to read)
*   تَجْلِسُ عَائِشَةُ ...... لِتَقْرَأَ (Aisha sits... to read)
*   أَنْتَ تَجْلِسُ ...... لِتَقْرَأَ (You [m.] sit... to read)
*   أَنْتِ تَجْلِسِينَ ...... لِتَقْرَئِي (You [f.] sit... to read)
*   أَنَا أَجْلِسُ ...... لِأَقْرَأَ (I sit... to read)
