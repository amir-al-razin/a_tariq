### **Verb Table (الأفعال)**
This lesson introduces verbs formed on the pattern of **بَابُ الْمُفَاعَلَةِ** (Form III), which generally denotes interacting or doing something with someone else. The primary example verb is **الْمُسَاعَدَةُ** (to help) [3]:

**Past Tense (الماضي):**
*   **سَاعَدَ** (He helped)
*   **سَاعَدَتْ** (She helped)
*   **سَاعَدْتَ** (You [masculine] helped)
*   **سَاعَدْتِ** (You [feminine] helped)
*   **سَاعَدْتُ** (I helped)

**Present/Future Tense (المضارع):**
*   **يُسَاعِدُ** (He helps / will help)
*   **تُسَاعِدُ** (She helps / will help)
*   **تُسَاعِدُ** (You [masculine] help / will help)
*   **تُسَاعِدِيْنَ** (You [feminine] help / will help)
*   **أُسَاعِدُ** (I help / will help)

**Command (الأمر) & Prohibition (النهي):**
*   **سَاعِدْ** (Help! — *Command to a male*)
*   **سَاعِدِي** (Help! — *Command to a female*)
*   **لَا تُسَاعِدْ** (Do not help! — *Prohibition to a male*)
*   **لَا تُسَاعِدِي** (Do not help! — *Prohibition to a female*)
