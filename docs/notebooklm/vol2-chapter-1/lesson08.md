**Exercise 2: Fill in the Blanks** [2]
The exercise requires turning the phrase into a vocative expression by adding **يَا** (O), which forces the Mudaf (the first word) into the Mansub state (taking a Fatha ending).

*   **مُعَلِّمُ الْمَدْرَسَةِ** (Teacher of the school) 
    *   *Answer:* **يَا مُعَلِّمَ الْمَدْرَسَةِ** (O teacher of the school)
*   **إِمَامُ الْمَسْجِدِ** (Imam of the mosque)
    *   *Answer:* **يَا إِمَامَ الْمَسْجِدِ** (O Imam of the mosque)
*   **صَدِيقُ مَحْمُودٍ** (Mahmud's friend)
    *   *Answer:* **يَا صَدِيقَ مَحْمُودٍ** (O Mahmud's friend)
*   **صَدِيقَةُ زَيْنَبَ** (Zainab's friend [fem.])
    *   *Answer:* **يَا صَدِيقَةَ زَيْنَبَ** (O Zainab's friend)
*   **أَخُو بِلَالٍ** (Bilal's brother)
    *   *Answer:* **يَا أَخَا بِلَالٍ** (O Bilal's brother) *(Note: أَخُو is from the special five nouns, so its Mansub form takes an Alif).*
