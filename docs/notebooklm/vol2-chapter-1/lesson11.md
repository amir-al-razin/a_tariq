### **Vocabulary**
The lesson introduces two new particles and their meanings:
*   **إِنَّ (Inna):** Certainly / Indeed / Surely [1].
*   **لَعَلَّ (La'alla):** Perhaps / Maybe [1].

### **Grammar and Sentence Structure**
The lesson demonstrates how the particle **إِنَّ** changes the subject of a nominal sentence to take a *fatha* (accusative case/Mansub) [1]:
*   رَاشِدٌ تِلْمِيْذٌ (Rashed is a student) ➔ **إِنَّ رَاشِدًا تِلْمِيْذٌ** (Indeed, Rashed is a student) [1].
*   عَائِشَةُ تِلْمِيْذَةٌ (Aisha is a female student) ➔ **إِنَّ عَائِشَةَ تِلْمِيْذَةٌ** (Indeed, Aisha is a female student) [1].
*   الكِتَابُ جَدِيْدٌ (The book is new) ➔ **إِنَّ الكِتَابَ جَدِيْدٌ** (Indeed, the book is new) [1].
*   هَذِهِ السَّاعَةُ غَالِيَةٌ (This watch is expensive) ➔ **إِنَّ هَذِهِ السَّاعَةَ غَالِيَةٌ** (Indeed, this watch is expensive) [1].
*   عَمُّ مَاجِدٍ عَالِمٌ (Majid's uncle is a scholar) ➔ **إِنَّ عَمَّ مَاجِدٍ عَالِمٌ** (Indeed, Majid's uncle is a scholar) [1].
