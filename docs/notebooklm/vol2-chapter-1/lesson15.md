### Verb Tables: بَابُ التَّفَعُّلِ
Using the root verbal noun **التَّعَلُّمُ** (To learn) [1]:

**Past Tense (الماضي):**
*   تَعَلَّمَ (He learned)
*   تَعَلَّمَتْ (She learned)
*   تَعَلَّمْتَ (You [m] learned)
*   تَعَلَّمْتِ (You [f] learned)
*   تَعَلَّمْتُ (I learned)

**Present/Future Tense (المضارع):**
*   يَتَعَلَّمُ (He learns/will learn)
*   تَتَعَلَّمُ (She learns/will learn)
*   تَتَعَلَّمُ (You [m] learn/will learn)
*   تَتَعَلَّمِينَ (You [f] learn/will learn)
*   أَتَعَلَّمُ (I learn/will learn)

**Command (الأمر):**
*   تَعَلَّمْ (Learn! [m])
*   تَعَلَّمِي (Learn! [f])

**Prohibition (النهي):**
*   لا تَتَعَلَّمْ (Do not learn! [m])
*   لا تَتَعَلَّمِي (Do not learn! [f])

### Exercises / Grammar Usage Note
The lesson concludes with an exercise pointing out the difference in usage between two similar verbs that mean "to speak to/with" [2]:

*   **كَلَّمْتُ رَاشِدًا** (I spoke to Rashed) is grammatically compared with **تَكَلَّمْتُ مَعَ رَاشِدٍ** (I spoke with Rashed) [2].
*   **أُكَلِّمُهُ** (I will speak to him) is grammatically compared with **أَتَكَلَّمُ مَعَهُ** (I will speak with him) [2].
