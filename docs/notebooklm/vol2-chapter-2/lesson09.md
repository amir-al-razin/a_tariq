**GRAMMAR_RULE**
**Rule 1: Pluralizing Non-Human Nouns** [1]
When transforming a sentence with a singular non-human noun into a plural, the associated adjectives and demonstrative pronouns change to singular feminine (e.g., هَذَا كِتَابٌ جَدِيدٌ becomes هَذِهِ كُتُبٌ جَدِيدَةٌ) [1]. The lesson demonstrates sentence progression applying these rules: الكِتَابُ جَدِيدٌ (The book is new) scales to الكُتُبُ جَدِيدَةٌ (The books are new) [1]. 

**Rule 2: Diptotes (Colors) with the Definite Article** [4]
Color adjectives (like أَسْوَد, أَبْيَض, زَرْقَاء, بَيْضَاء) are diptotes and normally take a *fatha* in the genitive/prepositional state (مجرور) [4]. However, when prefixed with the definite article "ال", they accept a standard *kasrah* [4]. 
*Examples:*
*   يَطِيرُ فِي سَمَاءٍ زَرْقَاءَ (It flies in a blue sky) -> يَطِيرُ فِي السَّمَاءِ الزَّرْقَاءِ (It flies in the blue sky) [4].
*   اكْتُبْ بِقَلَمٍ أَسْوَدَ (Write with a black pen) -> اكْتُبْ بِالقَلَمِ الأَسْوَدِ (Write with the black pen) [4].
*   دَخَلَتِ الحَدِيقَةَ لِوَرْدَةٍ بَيْضَاءَ (She entered the garden for a white rose) -> دَخَلَتِ الحَدِيقَةَ لِلوَرْدَةِ البَيْضَاءِ (She entered the garden for the white rose) [4].
*   كَتَبْتُ عَلَى وَرَقٍ أَبْيَضَ (I wrote on white paper) -> كَتَبْتُ عَلَى الوَرَقِ الأَبْيَضِ (I wrote on the white paper) [4].
