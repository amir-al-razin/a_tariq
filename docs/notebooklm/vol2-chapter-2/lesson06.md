### GRAMMAR_RULE
**1. The Interpreted Verbal Noun (المَصْدَر المُؤَوَّل):**
This lesson teaches how to use the particle **أَنْ** (that) followed by a present tense verb (الفِعْل المُضَارِع) to create the meaning of a verbal noun (masdar). 
*   *Example:* **عَمَلُكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** (Your work is better for you) is grammatically equivalent in meaning to **أَنْ تَعْمَلُوا خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** (That you work is better for you). 
*   *Example:* **صَوْمُكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** (Your fast is better for you) is equivalent to **أَنْ تَصُومُوا خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ** (That you fast is better for you).

**2. The Contraction of أَنْ and لَا:**
When **أَنْ** (that) is immediately followed by the negative particle **لَا** (not), they merge into one word: **أَلَّا** (That not).

**3. Usage of لَمَّا and عِنْدَمَا (When):**
*   **لَمَّا** (Lammaa) is only used with the Past Tense (الماضي). Example: لَمَّا سَمِعَ (When he heard...).
*   **عِنْدَمَا** ('Indamaa) can be used with both the Past Tense and the Present Tense (المضارع). Example: عِنْدَمَا سَمِعَ (When he heard...) and عِنْدَمَا يَسْمَعُ (When he hears...).

*(He who knows the value of time is better than he who knows the value of wealth.)*
(٢) الَّذِينَ يَعْرِفُونَ قِيمَةَ الوَقْتِ خَيْرٌ مِمَّنْ يَعْرِفُونَ قِيمَةَ المَالِ .
*(Those who know the value of time are better than those who know the value of wealth.)*
(٣) التِي تَعْرِفُ قِيمَةَ الوَقْتِ خَيْرٌ مِمَّنْ تَعْرِفُ قِيمَةَ المَالِ .
*(She who knows the value of time is better than she who knows the value of wealth.)*
(٤) خُذْ مَا أَعْطَاكَ رَبُّكَ شَاكِرًا . خُذُوا مَا أَعْطَاكُمْ رَبُّكُمْ شَاكِرِينَ . خُذْنَ مَا أَعْطَاكُنَّ رَبُّكُنَّ شَاكِرَاتٍ .
*(Take what your Lord has given you thankfully [Addressing: singular male / plural males / plural females].)*
