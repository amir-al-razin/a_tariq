### **GRAMMAR_RULE**
**Conjugation of Doubled Verbs in Form IV (إِفْعَال - أَفْعَلَ):**
This lesson introduces the plural conjugations for Form IV verbs that have a doubled root letter (الْفِعْلُ الْمُضَاعَفُ), such as **أَحَبَّ** (to love), **أَعَدَّ** (to prepare), and **أَتَمَّ** (to complete) [1, 2]. 
*   **Rule:** When conjugating these verbs for feminine plural pronouns (هُنَّ, أَنْتُنَّ) or attaching subject pronouns that begin with a consonant in the past tense (أَنْتُمْ, نَحْنُ), the merged double letter must be separated (فك الإدغام). 
*   **Example:** أَحَبُّوا (They loved) has the letters merged, but for the feminine plural it becomes أَحْبَبْنَ (They [fem.] loved) where the 'ب' is split into two [1].

### **VERB_TABLE**
Memorize the following verb chain for the verb **أَحَبَّ** (To love) with its plural subject pronouns [1]:

**Past Tense (الماضي):**
*   **أَحَبُّوا** - They (m.pl.) loved
*   **أَحْبَبْنَ** - They (f.pl.) loved
*   **أَحْبَبْتُمْ** - You (m.pl.) loved
*   **أَحْبَبْتُنَّ** - You (f.pl.) loved
*   **أَحْبَبْنَا** - We loved

**Present Tense (المضارع):**
*   **يُحِبُّونَ** - They (m.pl.) love
*   **يُحْبِبْنَ** - They (f.pl.) love
*   **تُحِبُّونَ** - You (m.pl.) love
*   **تُحْبِبْنَ** - You (f.pl.) love
*   **نُحِبُّ** - We love

**Imperative & Prohibitive (الأمر والنهي):**
*   **أَحِبُّوا** - Love! (m.pl.)
*   **أَحْبِبْنَ** - Love! (f.pl.)
*   **لَا تُحِبُّوا** - Do not love! (m.pl.)
*   **لَا تُحْبِبْنَ** - Do not love! (f.pl.)

### **PARAGRAPH**
**Reading Passage 1:**
> قَالَ العَالِمُ : أَيُّهَا الْمُسْلِمُونَ ! أَعِدُّوا لِلْمُشْرِكِينَ قُوَّةً تُرْهِبُونَ بِهَا عَدُوَّ اللهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمْ . قَالَ اللهُ تَعَالَى فِي القُرْآنِ : أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَنْ يَغْفِرَ اللهُ لَكُمْ .
**Translation:** The scholar said: O Muslims! Prepare a force against the polytheists by which you terrify the enemy of Allah and your enemy. Allah the Almighty said in the Quran: "Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?" [1].

**Translation:** The father said to his children: I ask you to complete this work in three hours. Majid said: O my friends! I heard that you have prepared a valuable article that you will publish in the Voice of Islam newspaper? The friends said: O Majid! We are striving to prepare it. The scholar said: O Muslims! **Translation:** We love the spring season, because it is the season of beauty and flowers. We love to spend the spring season in our village to witness the beauty of spring and praise Allah who created this beauty [2].

**Reading Passage 5:**
