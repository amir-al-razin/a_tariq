**Conjugation of Defective Verbs (الأفعال الناقصة) for Plural Pronouns**
This lesson introduces the plural conjugations (for هُمْ، هُنَّ، أَنْتُمْ، أَنْتُنَّ، نَحْنُ) of defective verbs—verbs whose final root letter is a weak letter (و، ي، أ). The rules differ slightly based on the root's weak letter and the middle vowel:
1. **Waw-root verbs (e.g., دَعَا):** The weak letter is dropped for masculine plurals but retained for feminine plurals. 
2. **Ya-root verbs (e.g., بَكَى):** The weak letter is dropped for masculine plurals, taking a Kasrah before the Waw, but retained for feminine plurals.
3. **Kasrah middle-root verbs (e.g., نَسِيَ):** The Alif Maqsura is dropped for masculine plurals (taking a Fathah before the Waw) and reverts to Ya for feminine plurals.
4. **Fathah middle-root verbs (e.g., سَعَى):** The Alif Maqsura is dropped for masculine plurals (taking a Fathah before the Waw) and reverts to Ya for feminine plurals.

[VERB_TABLE]
**Group 1: Waw-Root Verbs (Pattern: دَعَا - يَدْعُو)**
*Verbs in this group:* دَعَا (to call), تَلَا (to recite), رَجَا (to hope), نَجَا (to survive/escape), عَفَا (to forgive).
*   **Past Plural:** دَعَوْا (They call - M) - دَعَوْنَ (They call - F) - دَعَوْتُمْ (You call - M) - دَعَوْتُنَّ (You call - F) - دَعَوْنَا (We call)
*   **Present Plural:** يَدْعُونَ (They call - M) - يَدْعُونَ (They call - F) - تَدْعُونَ (You call - M) - تَدْعُونَ (You call - F) - نَدْعُو (We call)
*   **Imperative Plural:** اُدْعُوا (Call! - M) - اُدْعُونَ (Call! - F) - لَا تَدْعُوا (Do not call! - M) - لَا تَدْعُونَ (Do not call! - F)

**Group 2: Ya-Root Verbs (Pattern: بَكَى - يَبْكِي)**
*Verbs in this group:* بَكَى (to cry), مَشَى (to walk), سَقَى (to give drink), نَوَى (to intend), بَنَى (to build).
*   **Past Plural:** بَكَوْا (They cried - M) - بَكَيْنَ (They cried - F) - بَكَيْتُمْ (You cried - M) - بَكَيْتُنَّ (You cried - F) - بَكَيْنَا (We cried)
*   **Present Plural:** يَبْكُونَ (They cry - M) - يَبْكِينَ (They cry - F) - تَبْكُونَ (You cry - M) - تَبْكِينَ (You cry - F) - نَبْكِي (We cry)
*   **Imperative Plural:** اِبْكُوا (Cry! - M) - اِبْكِينَ (Cry! - F) - لَا تَبْكُوا (Do not cry! - M) - لَا تَبْكِينَ (Do not cry! - F)

**Group 3: Kasrah Middle-Root Verbs (Pattern: نَسِيَ - يَنْسَى)**
*Verbs in this group:* نَسِيَ (to forget), رَضِيَ (to be pleased), بَقِيَ (to remain), خَشِيَ (to fear), لَقِيَ (to meet).
*   **Past Plural:** نَسُوا (They forgot - M) - نَسِينَ (They forgot - F) - نَسِيتُمْ (You forgot - M) - نَسِيتُنَّ (You forgot - F) - نَسِينَا (We forgot)
*   **Present Plural:** يَنْسَوْنَ (They forget - M) - يَنْسَيْنَ (They forget - F) - تَنْسَوْنَ (You forget - M) - تَنْسَيْنَ (You forget - F) - نَنْسَى (We forget)
*   **Imperative Plural:** اِنْسَوْا (Forget! - M) - اِنْسَيْنَ (Forget! - F) - لَا تَنْسَوْا (Do not forget! - M) - لَا تَنْسَيْنَ (Do not forget! - F)

**Group 4: Fathah Middle-Root Verbs (Pattern: سَعَى - يَسْعَى)**
*Verbs in this group:* سَعَى (to strive), نَهَى (to forbid), أَبَى (to refuse), رَعَى (to graze/care).
*   **Past Plural:** سَعَوْا (They strove - M) - سَعَيْنَ (They strove - F) - سَعَيْتُمْ (You strove - M) - سَعَيْتُنَّ (You strove - F) - سَعَيْنَا (We strove)
*   **Present Plural:** يَسْعَوْنَ (They strive - M) - يَسْعَيْنَ (They strive - F) - تَسْعَوْنَ (You strive - M) - تَسْعَيْنَ (You strive - F) - نَسْعَى (We strive)
*   **Imperative Plural:** اِسْعَوْا (Strive! - M) - اِسْعَيْنَ (Strive! - F) - لَا تَسْعَوْا (Do not strive! - M) - لَا تَسْعَيْنَ (Do not strive! - F)

**Using "كَانَ" for Past Habitual Actions**
When `كَانَ` (to be) is followed by a present tense verb (فعل مضارع), it expresses a continuous or habitual action in the past (used to do / was doing). The subject can be placed before or after `كَانَ`, which changes its conjugation. 
Example:
*   كَانَ الْأَنْبِيَاءُ يَرْعَوْنَ الْغَنَمَ (The prophets used to graze sheep)
*   الْأَنْبِيَاءُ كَانُوا يَرْعَوْنَ الْغَنَمَ (The prophets, they used to graze sheep)
*   كَانَ يَرْعَى الْأَنْبِيَاءُ الْغَنَمَ (The prophets used to graze sheep - placing the verb directly after Kana)

**Using أَمَّا ... فَـ (As for... then...)**
This structure is used to contrast two subjects or concepts. 
*   أَنْتَ ذَكِيٌّ ، أَمَّا أَخُوكَ فَغَبِيٌّ . (You are smart, as for your brother, he is stupid).
*   الْبَخِيلُ يَجْمَعُ الْمَالَ، أَمَّا السَّخِيُّ فَيُنْفِقُ مَالَهُ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ . (The miser hoards wealth, as for the generous one, he spends his wealth in the path of Allah).

**Relative Pronouns (الأسماء الموصولة)**
*   الَّذِي (He who / The one who - Sing. M)
*   الَّتِي (She who / The one who - Sing. F)
*   الَّذِينَ (Those who - Pl. M)
*   اللَّاتِي (Those who - Pl. F)
They link dependent clauses to nouns. They can act as subjects or objects:
*   الَّذِي يَصْدُقُ يَنْجُو (He who speaks the truth survives).
*   أُدْعُ الَّذِي يَصْدُقُ (Call the one who speaks the truth).
