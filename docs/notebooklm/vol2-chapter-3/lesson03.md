**[GRAMMAR_RULE]**
1. **The Dual Form (المثنى):** Nouns and adjectives are made dual by adding the suffix "ـَانِ" (aan) to the singular form. For example, "مُعَلِّمٌ" (one teacher) becomes "مُعَلِّمَانِ" (two teachers). The pronouns for the dual are "هُمَا" (they two), "أَنْتُمَا" (you two), and "نَحْنُ" (we two) [1].
2. **Plurals of Nouns and Demonstratives (الجمع):** The lesson introduces plural pronouns "هَؤُلَاءِ" (These) and "أُولَئِكَ" (Those) to be used with plural human nouns like "رِجَالٌ" (men) and "نِسَاءٌ" (women). Non-human plurals are treated as feminine singular [2]. 
3. **The Comparative Adjective (اسم التفضيل):** Formed on the pattern of "أَفْعَلُ" (Af'alu), often followed by "مِنْ" (than). For example, "أَكْبَرُ مِنْ" (bigger than), "أَصْغَرُ مِنْ" (smaller than), "أَوْسَعُ مِنْ" (wider than) [2].
4. **Absolute Negation (لا النافية للجنس):** Using "لَا" followed by a noun ending in a single Fatha (Fat'ha) to mean "Absolutely no...". For example, "لَا شَكَّ" (There is no doubt) [3].
