**GRAMMAR_RULE**
1. **Relative Pronouns (اَلْأَسْمَاءُ الْمَوْصُولَة):** This lesson focuses heavily on relative pronouns used to link clauses and provide descriptions. 
*   **اَلَّذِي** : Who / Which / That (Singular Masculine)
*   **اَلَّتِي** : Who / Which / That (Singular Feminine & Non-human Plural)
*   **اَلَّذِينَ** : Who (Plural Masculine, rational/human)
*   **اَللَّاتِي** : Who (Plural Feminine, rational/human)
2. **The Universal Relative Pronouns "مَنْ" and "مَا":** 
*   **مَنْ** (Who / Whoever): Used interchangeably for humans/rational beings regardless of their gender and number (e.g., مَنْ يَعْمَلُ بِعِلْمِهِ - *Whoever acts upon his knowledge*). 
*   **مَا** (What / That which / Whatever): Used for non-human entities and inanimate objects (e.g., مَا يَنْفَعُكَ - *What benefits you*).
