Here is the complete breakdown of Volume 3, Chapter 3, Lesson 6 (الباب الثالث: الدرس السادس), focusing on the introduction of two new verb forms (Baabs): **Bab Al-Infi'aal (بَابُ الانْفِعَالِ)** and **Bab At-Tafaa'ul (بَابُ التَّفَاعُلِ)** [1, 2].

**VOCABULARY**
*   الاِنْفِتَاح (Al-Infitāḥ) - Opening / getting opened [1]
*   الاِنْكِسَار (Al-Inkisār) - Breaking / getting broken [1]
*   الاِنْهِدَام (Al-Inhidām) - Collapsing / getting destroyed [1]
*   الاِنْكِشَاف (Al-Inkishāf) - Revealing / getting uncovered [1]
*   التَّمَارُض (At-Tamāruḍ) - Feigning sickness [2]
*   التَّنَاوُم (At-Tanāwum) - Feigning sleep [2]
*   التَّشَاوُر (At-Tashāwur) - Mutual consultation [2]
*   التَّقَاتُل (At-Taqātul) - Mutual fighting [2]
*   التَّحَادُث (At-Taḥāduth) - Mutual conversation [2]
*   التَّعَارُف (At-Taʿāruf) - Mutual acquaintance [2]
*   مُخْطِئٌ (Mukhṭiʾun) - Mistaken / Wrong [2]

**GRAMMAR_RULE**
*   **Bab Al-Infi'aal (بَابُ الاِنْفِعَالِ):** This verb pattern typically conveys the passive or intransitive consequence of an action. When a transitive action is done to an object, this form expresses the state change of that object (e.g., "I broke it" -> "It broke") [1].
*   **Bab At-Tafaa'ul (بَابُ التَّفَاعُلِ):** This verb pattern has two distinct characteristics introduced in this lesson:
    1.  **Feigning/Artificiality (কৃত্রিমতার অর্থ প্রকাশ করা):** It expresses pretending to do an action or feigning a state (e.g., pretending to be asleep) [1, 2].
    2.  **Mutuality/Reciprocity (পরস্পরতার অর্থ প্রকাশ করা):** It expresses an action done reciprocally between two or more parties. Because it is mutual, the doer (فَاعِل - subject) must be more than one person (dual or plural) [2].

**APPLICATION**
*Bab Al-Infi'aal (Consequence of Action)* [1]:
*   فَتَحْتُ الْبَابَ (I opened the door) -> اِنْفَتَحَ الْبَابُ (The door opened).
*   كَسَرَ الْوَلَدُ الزُّجَاجَ (The boy broke the glass) -> اِنْكَسَرَ الزُّجَاجُ (The glass broke).
*   كَشَفَ الرَّجُلُ الْحَقِيقَةَ (The man revealed the truth) -> اِنْكَشَفَتِ الْحَقِيقَةُ (The truth was revealed).
*   هَدَمَ الْبَيْتَ (He destroyed the house) -> اِنْهَدَمَ الْبَيْتُ (The house collapsed).

*Bab At-Tafaa'ul (Feigning State)* [1]:
*   مَا مَرِضَ الرَّجُلُ بَلْ تَمَارَضَ (The man did not get sick, but he feigned sickness).
*   مَا نَامَ الرَّجُلُ بَلْ تَنَاوَمَ (The man did not sleep, but he feigned sleep).

*Bab At-Tafaa'ul (Mutuality)* [2]:
*   تَشَاوَرَ الرَّجُلَانِ (The two men consulted each other).
*   تَقَاتَلَ الرَّجُلَانِ (The two men fought each other).

**PARAGRAPH**
إِنْ قُلْتَ : تَحَادَثَ الرَّجُلُ فَأَنْتَ مُخْطِئٌ، لِأَنَّ هَذَا الْفِعْلَ يَطْلُبُ فَاعِلَيْنِ أَوْ أَكْثَرَ . فَيَجِبُ أَنْ تَقُولَ : تَحَادَثَ الرَّجُلَانِ . أَرْجُو أَنَّكَ قَدْ فَهِمْتَ الْأَمْرَ فَهْمًا جَيِّدًا . فَمَاذَا تَقُولُ عَنْ : تَعَارَفَ الْوَلَدُ ؟ [2]

*(English Translation)*: If you say: "The man conversed (mutually)", you are wrong, because this verb requires two subjects or more. So you must say: "The two men conversed". I hope that you have understood the matter very well. So what do you say about: "The boy got acquainted (mutually)"? [2]

**QA**
*   **Q:** لِمَاذَا تَكُونُ جُمْلَةُ "تَحَادَثَ الرَّجُلُ" خَاطِئَةً؟ (Why is the sentence "The man conversed (mutually)" wrong?) [2]
*   **A:** لِأَنَّ هَذَا الْفِعْلَ يَطْلُبُ فَاعِلَيْنِ أَوْ أَكْثَرَ. (Because this verb requires two subjects or more) [2].

**ASSESSMENT**
*   **Question:** فَمَاذَا تَقُولُ عَنْ : تَعَارَفَ الْوَلَدُ ؟ (What do you say about the sentence: "The boy got acquainted (mutually)"?) [2]
*   **Expected Answer:** This sentence is grammatically incorrect based on the rules of *Bab At-Tafaa'ul* regarding mutuality. Because getting mutually acquainted requires more than one person, the subject must be dual or plural. It should be corrected to: تَعَارَفَ الْوَلَدَانِ (The two boys got acquainted).

**MASDAR_FACTORY**
*   Base pattern for *Infi'aal*: اِنْفِعَال (e.g., اِنْفِتَاح، اِنْكِسَار) [1].
*   Base pattern for *Tafaa'ul*: تَفَاعُل (e.g., تَمَارُض، تَنَاوُم، تَشَاوُر) [2].
