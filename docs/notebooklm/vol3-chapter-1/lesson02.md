**GRAMMAR_RULE**
*   **Negating the Past Tense (فِعْل مَاضٍ):** To negate a past tense verb, place the particle `مَا` before it [4]. Examples include مَا فَعَلَ (He did not do), مَا فَعَلَتْ (She did not do), and مَا فَعَلْتُ (I did not do) [4].
*   **Negating the Present/Future Tense (فِعْل مُضَارِع):** To negate a present/future tense verb, place the particle `لا` before it [5]. Examples include لا يَفْعَلُ (He does not/will not do), لا تَفْعَلُ (She does not/will not do), and لا أَفْعَلُ (I do not/will not do) [5].
*   **Answering Positive vs. Negative Questions:** When answering a standard positive question, respond with `نَعَمْ` (Yes) to affirm, or `لا` (No) to negate [6]. When responding to a negative question (e.g., Didn't you go?), answer with `بَلَى` (Yes/Indeed) to affirm that the action *was* done, or `نَعَمْ` (Yes) to agree with the negative premise that the action was *not* done [6].
