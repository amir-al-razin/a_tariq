#### **4. Grammar Rules (قواعد النحو)**
The implicit grammar rule taught throughout this specific lesson is **Noun-Adjective Agreement (Mawsuf and Sifat)**. The lesson emphasizes that the descriptive adjective (Sifat) must follow and match the noun (Mawsuf) in gender (e.g., using masculine `طَازَجٌ` with `لَحْمٌ`, but feminine `طَازَجَةٌ` with `سَمَكَةٌ`) [1].

#### **5. General Questions vs. Special Questions**
At the end of the lesson, a specific box is provided with the note: *"শুধু বোঝার জন্য, অনুশীলনের জন্য নয়"* (Just for understanding, not for practice) [5]. It explicitly maps out the structural difference between asking a yes/no question versus an alternative "either/or" question.

**سؤال عام (General Question - Using هَلْ):**
These are simple confirming questions answered with yes (نَعَمْ) or no (لَا).
*   هَلْ هَذَا مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ ؟ نَعَمْ .. هَذَا مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ .
    *(Is this a big key? Yes.. this is a big key.)*
*   هَلْ هَذَا مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ ؟ لَا .. هَذَا مِفْتَاحٌ صَغِيرٌ .
    *(Is this a big key? No.. this is a small key.)* [5]

**سؤال خاص (Special Question - Using أَ... أَمْ):**
These questions ask the listener to specify between two attributes.
*   أَمِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ هَذَا أَمْ مِفْتَاحٌ صَغِيرٌ ؟ مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ .
    *(Is this a big key, or a small key? A big key.)*
*   أَمِفْتَاحٌ صَغِيرٌ هَذَا أَمْ مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ ؟ مِفْتَاحٌ كَبِيرٌ .
    *(Is this a small key, or a big key? A big key.)* [5]
