### **2. Grammar Rules (القواعد): Negating Adjectives with 'Ghayr' (غَيْرُ)**
The lesson introduces the grammatical structure for creating negative adjectives using **غَيْرُ** (non-/un-), which acts as a *Mudaf* (possessed) and makes the following adjective *Majrur* (genitive case) [3]:
*   مَاهِرٌ (দক্ষ) — غَيْرُ مَاهِرٍ (অদক্ষ) [3]
*   مَشْهُورٌ (প্রসিদ্ধ) — غَيْرُ مَشْهُورٍ (অপ্রসিদ্ধ) [3]
*   لَذِيْذٌ (সুস্বাদু) — غَيْرُ لَذِيْذٍ (সুস্বাদু নয়) [3]

**Application in Sentences:**
*   رَاشِدٌ لَاعِبٌ مَاهِرٌ وَ أَنْتَ لَاعِبٌ غَيْرُ مَاهِرٍ (Rashed is a skilled player and you are an unskilled player) [3].
*   هَذِهِ سَمَكَةٌ لَذِيْذَةٌ وَ تِلْكَ سَمَكَةٌ غَيْرُ لَذِيْذَةٍ (This is a tasty fish and that is a non-tasty fish) [3].
*   هَذَا قِطَارٌ سَرِيْعٌ وَ ذَلِكَ قِطَارٌ غَيْرُ سَرِيْعٍ (This is a fast train and that is a non-fast train) [3].

### **3. General Questions vs. Special Questions (سؤال عام / سؤال خاص)**
A major pedagogical focus of this lesson is teaching students the difference between standard Yes/No questions and alternative questions [3-5]. 

**General Question (সাধারণ প্রশ্ন) using Hal (هَلْ):**
*   هَلْ أَنْتَ مُسْلِمٌ ؟ نَعَمْ .. أَنَا مُسْلِمٌ (Are you a Muslim? Yes, I am a Muslim.) [3]
*   هَلْ هُوَ مُعَلِّمٌ ؟ لَا .. هُوَ تِلْمِيْذٌ (Is he a teacher? No, he is a student.) [3]

**Special Question (বিশেষ প্রশ্ন) using A...am (أَ ... أَمْ):**
The book divides this into two progressive steps [4, 5]:
*   *Without 'Am' (أم ছাড়া):* أَ مُسْلِمٌ أَنْتَ ؟ نَعَمْ .. أَنَا مُسْلِمٌ (Are you a Muslim? Yes, I am a Muslim.) [4]
*   *With 'Am' (أم সহ):* أَ مُسْلِمٌ أَنْتَ أَمْ مُشْرِكٌ ؟ مُسْلِمٌ (Are you a Muslim or a polytheist? A Muslim.) [4]
*   أَ تِلْمِيْذٌ خَالِدٌ أَمْ مُعَلِّمٌ ؟ مُعَلِّمٌ (Is Khaled a student or a teacher? A teacher.) [4]

### **4. Image-Indication Examples**
To reinforce the question structures, the lesson uses visual cues (a pen and a ruler) with the following drill [4]:

*   *(Special with Am)* أَ قَلَمٌ هَذَا أَمْ مِسْطَرَةٌ ؟ قَلَمٌ (Is this a pen or a ruler? A pen.) [4]

The lesson provides reading passages to test the vocabulary and grammar [1, 2]:

*   **التمرين الثالث (Exercise 3):** Speaking prompts requiring students to generate their own sentences (e.g., "Say 3 sentences about yourself as a businessman," "Say 3 sentences addressing Khadija who is rich and generous.") [3].
