### 2. Grammar Rules & Foundational Examples (القواعد والأمثلة)
The pedagogical focus is on making the adjective agree with the noun's gender. A masculine noun takes a masculine adjective, and a feminine noun (typically ending in ة) takes a feminine adjective [1]:
*   كِتَابٌ جَدِيدٌ (একটি নতুন বই - A new book) [1]
*   كُرَّاسَةٌ جَدِيدَةٌ (একটি নতুন খাতা - A new notebook) [1]
*   هَذَا كِتَابٌ جَدِيدٌ (ইহা (একটি) নতুন বই ! - This is a new book!) [2]
