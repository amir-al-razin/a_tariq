Based on the provided sources, Chapter 1, Lesson 1 (الدرس الأول - প্রথম পাঠ) spans from source [1] to source [2]. The lesson is carefully structured to introduce basic Arabic vocabulary alongside fundamental grammar concepts (demonstrative pronouns and simple interrogatives). 

Here is the comprehensive breakdown of the pedagogical structure and content of this lesson:

### 1. Initial Vocabulary Segment (Masculine Nouns)
The lesson begins by introducing eight basic masculine nouns with their Bengali translations [1]:
*   **كِتَابٌ** (kitabun) - একটি বই (A book)
*   **قَلَمٌ** (qalamun) - একটি কলম (A pen)
*   **كُرْسِيٌّ** (kursiyyun) - একটি চেয়ার (A chair)
*   **بَيْتٌ** (baytun) - একটি ঘর (A house)
*   **بَابٌ** (babun) - একটি দরজা (A door)
*   **مِصْبَاحٌ** (misbahun) - একটি বাতি (A lamp)
*   **جِدَارٌ** (jidarun) - একটি দেয়াল (A wall)
*   **سَرِيْرٌ** (sarirun) - একটি খাট (A bed)

### 2. Core Grammar Rule: Masculine Demonstrative Pronouns
Immediately following the vocabulary, the text introduces the grammatical structure for pointing to near and far objects (masculine) [1]:
*   **هَذَا** (hadha) - ইহা (এটি) [This]
*   **ذَلِكَ** (dhalika) - উহা (সেটি) [That]
*   **Examples:** The text combines the new grammar and vocabulary into basic sentences: **هَذَا كِتَابٌ** (This is a book) and **ذَلِكَ قَلَمٌ** (That is a pen) [1].

### 3. Application via Images and Translations (Part 1)
To reinforce the first vocabulary and grammar segments, the text provides a series of illustrated examples showing an object alongside its corresponding Arabic phrase indicating whether it is "this" or "that" [1, 3]. 
*   *Note: Through these images, a new word is implicitly introduced: **مَسْجِدٌ** (masjidun - Mosque)* [1, 3]. 
*   Examples include images paired with phrases like: **هَذَا بَابٌ** (This is a door), **ذَلِكَ مِصْبَاحٌ** (That is a lamp), **هَذَا جِدَارٌ** (This is a wall), and **ذَلِكَ كُرْسِيٌّ** (That is a chair) [3].

### 4. Secondary Vocabulary Segment (Feminine Nouns)
The lesson then advances to introduce feminine nouns (words ending in *Ta-Marbuta*) [4]:
*   **مَدْرَسَةٌ** (madrasatun) - একটি মাদ্রাসা (A madrasa)
*   **سَبُّوْرَةٌ** (sabburatun) - একটি ব্ল্যাকবোর্ড (A blackboard)
*   **مِسْطَرَةٌ** (mistaratun) - একটি রুলার (A ruler)
*   **حَقِيْبَةٌ** (haqibatun) - একটি ব্যাগ (A bag)
*   **كُرَّاسَةٌ** (kurrasatun) - একটি খাতা (A notebook)
*   **طَاوِلَةٌ** (tawilatun) - একটি টেবিল (A table)
*   **حُجْرَةٌ - غُرْفَةٌ** (hujratun/ghurfatun) - একটি কামরা (A room)
*   **نَافِذَةٌ** (nafidhatun) - একটি জানালা (A window)

### 5. Expanded Grammar Rule: Feminine Demonstrative Pronouns
Along with the feminine nouns, the corresponding feminine demonstrative pronouns are introduced through visual examples [4]:
*   **هَذِهِ** (hadhihi) - This (feminine)
*   **تِلْكَ** (tilka) - That (feminine)

### 6. Application via Images and Translations (Part 2)
Similar to the first segment, images are used to practice the new feminine rules [4, 5]:
*   Examples include: **هَذِهِ مَدْرَسَةٌ** (This is a madrasa), **تِلْكَ سَبُّوْرَةٌ** (That is a blackboard), **هَذِهِ حُجْرَةٌ** (This is a room), and **تِلْكَ حَقِيْبَةٌ** (That is a bag) [4].

### 7. Tertiary Vocabulary Segment (Mixed)
A final block of eight new nouns (a mix of masculine and feminine) is provided [5]:
*   **قُفْلٌ** (quflun) - একটি তালা (A lock)
*   **مِفْتَاحٌ** (miftahun) - একটি চাবি (A key)
*   **صُنْدُوْقٌ** (sunduqun) - একটি বাক্স (A box)
*   **سَاعَةٌ** (sa'atun) - একটি ঘড়ি (A watch/clock)
*   **مِظَلَّةٌ** (midhallatun) - একটি ছাতা (An umbrella)
*   **نَظَّارَةٌ** (nadharatun) - একটি চশমা (Glasses)
*   **سَيَّارَةٌ** (sayyaratun) - একটি কার (A car)
*   **دَرَّاجَةٌ** (darrajatun) - একটি সাইকেল (A bicycle)

### 8. Interrogative Grammar Rule (Q&A Framework)
The lesson teaches the student how to form basic questions using **مَا** (What) [5]:
*   **مَا هَذَا - مَا هَذِهِ** (ইহা কী ? / এটি কী ?) [What is this?]
*   **مَا ذَلِكَ - مَا تِلْكَ** (উহা কী ? / সেটি কী ?) [What is that?]

### 9. Explicit Q&A Practice 
Under the directive "প্রশ্নোত্তরগুলো পড়ো ও অর্থ বলো" (Read the Q&A and say the meaning), the book provides full model Q&A examples with pictures [5]:
*   *Image of a book* -> **مَا هَذَا ؟** (What is this?) -> **هَذَا كِتَابٌ** (This is a book.)
*   *Image of a pen* -> **مَا ذَلِكَ ؟** (What is that?) -> **ذَلِكَ قَلَمٌ** (That is a pen.)
*   *Image of a ruler* -> **مَا هَذِهِ ؟** (What is this?) -> **هَذِهِ مِسْطَرَةٌ** (This is a ruler.)
*   *Image of a blackboard* -> **مَا تِلْكَ ؟** (What is that?) -> **تِلْكَ سَبُّوْرَةٌ** (That is a blackboard.)

### 10. Interactive Assessment and Practical Directive
The lesson concludes with an interactive exercise page titled "ছবি দেখে উত্তর দাও" (Answer by looking at the pictures) [2]. 
*   It provides a column of images next to incomplete Q&A pairs where the student is given the question (e.g., Image of a chair next to **مَا هَذَا ؟**) and an empty box where the student must provide the answer independently [2]. 
*   **Pedagogical Instruction:** At the very end, there is a practical classroom directive for students: "দু'জন দাঁড়াও এবং দূর ও নিকট থেকে বিভিন্ন বস্তুর দিকে হাতে ইশারা করে প্রশ্ন করো এবং উত্তর দাও।" (**Two people stand up and pointing at different objects from near and far, ask questions and give answers**) [2].
