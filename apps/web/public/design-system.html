PHILOSOPHY • RAW NEUTRAL V2

Tone-on-tone luminance depth. Zero decorative borders. Zero drop shadows.

The Tariq Design System creates visual depth exclusively through luminance shifts between layered flat surfaces. Designed for Quranic Arabic pedagogy, it eliminates visual noise so learners can focus entirely on Harakat (diacritics), linguistic harmony, and script geometry.

Rule 01

Pure Grayscale Base

Canvas is strictly #FAFAFA and surfaces are pure white #FFFFFF. Absolutely no yellowish or warm-stone tints.

Rule 02

Plane-on-Plane Hierarchy

Depth is achieved by placing a neutral-100 inset well inside a bg-white card. Zero 1px stroke outlines.

Rule 03

Plush Touch Ergonomics

Primary action buttons require generous vertical padding (py-[18px] min-h-[56px]) and full pill squircles.
